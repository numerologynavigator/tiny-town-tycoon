Tiny Town Tycoon Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This policy explains what Tiny Town Tycoon ("the game", made by Navrang Labs) does with information, in plain language.

What stays on your phone

Your towns, scores, level, streak, medals, best-towns gallery and your sound, music and vibration settings are saved in a file on your device. The game does not have accounts and does not send this file anywhere. If you use Android's backup, Android may copy it to your Google account under your own device backup settings.

Ads

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. Google's software in the game may collect and use information from your device to show ads and measure them. According to Google this can include your advertising ID, your IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, diagnostic information such as crash logs, and how you interact with ads. We do not receive your personal information from this; we see only summary reports.

  • Consent. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK) the game asks for your consent with Google's User Messaging Platform before requesting ads. You can revisit your choice in Settings > Ad privacy choices, which appears while ads are running.
  • Personalised ads. If you do not consent, you may still see ads that are not based on your interests.
  • Ad content. The game asks for ads suitable for general audiences (rating G).
  • Reset or disable your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).

More about how Google handles this: policies.google.com/privacy and support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543.

What the game does not do

  • No account, name, email or phone number is asked for.
  • No precise location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.
  • No in-app purchases and no payment information.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting service of our own.
  • No selling of your data.

Sharing a town

The share button creates a picture of your island with its score and seed code and hands it to Android's share menu. You choose where it goes. The picture contains no name, account, device or location information.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and is not designed for the Families program. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has used the game, contact us at the email below.

Your choices and rights

You can uninstall the game to remove its saved data from your device. Depending on where you live you may have rights over personal data held about you (access, deletion, objection). We do not hold personal data about you ourselves; for data held by Google through ads, use Google's own tools at myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy.
