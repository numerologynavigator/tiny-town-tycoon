Tiny Town Tycoon

Build a cosy toy town with one tap.

Coming soon to Google Play

A city builder you can learn in five seconds

Each turn you are offered three building cards. Tap a card, tap an empty tile, and a tiny toy building pops onto your island. Put buildings next to the right neighbours and they score bonus points. There is no timer, no fail screen, and you can undo your last move for free.

What you get

  • A cute 3D toy island with soft shadows, tiny walking citizens, chimney smoke, birds and a gentle day and night cycle.
  • A Daily Town: everyone gets the same cards in the same order each day, so you can compare seed codes with friends. A day streak is optional.
  • 40 buildings to collect in five sets: Town, Farm, Beach, Winter and Space.
  • Your island grows from 6x6 up to 10x10 as you level up.
  • One to three stars for every finished town, medals, and a gallery of your best towns kept on your phone.
  • Sound, music and vibration can each be switched off.

Ads and privacy

The game is free and contains ads. Rewarded videos are optional, and there are no in-app purchases. No account is needed, and your towns are saved on your device only. Read the privacy policy.
